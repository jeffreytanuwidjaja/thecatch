Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

The Catch Science Uploader ("the App") is an internal command-line tool used solely by the channel operators to publish YouTube Shorts to The Catch Science. It is not available to the public.

2. Data We Access

With your explicit consent via Google OAuth, the App accesses:

  • YouTube channel: Upload videos, set metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail), and publish.
  • OAuth tokens: Stored locally in token.pickle on the upload machine to avoid repeated sign-in.

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, analytics, or usage metrics.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • OAuth refresh tokens remain on the local machine only.
  • No data is sent to any third party except Google/YouTube APIs.
  • Video files and metadata are provided by the operator at upload time.

4. Your Rights

You may revoke access at any time via Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access. Deleting token.pickle also revokes local access.

Updates will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
